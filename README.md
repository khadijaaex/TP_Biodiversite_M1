##Belezma_Atlas_Cedar_Conservation
Atlas Cedar forest monitoring and conservation assessment project in semi-arid mountain ecosystems.

#Objectif:   
To track the health status and regeneration rate of the endangered Atlas Cedar (Cedrus atlantica) in semi-arid mountain ecosystems to support forest conservation strategies.

##Data:  
​The dataset includes field survey measurements of tree health, altitude, and natural seedling regeneration across three forest plots.

##Structure:  
-​data/ : Field survey dataset and variable explanations.

-​docs/ : Ecological assessment and conservation recommendations.
